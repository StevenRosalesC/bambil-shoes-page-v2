## Brand & Style

The brand personality is rooted in craftsmanship, warmth, and trust. It balances the rugged authenticity of leatherwork with the refined elegance of bespoke fashion. The UI evokes an emotional response of comfort and exclusivity, inviting the user into a digital atelier.

The design style is a blend of **Minimalism** and **Tactile Craft**. By utilizing generous whitespace, warm canvas tones, and photography of raw leather textures and artisanal work, the interface feels airy yet grounded.

## Colors

The palette is a curated selection of earth tones that mimic the raw materials of shoemaking:
- **Primary (#26170c / #3D2B1F):** Leather Brown. Used for high-contrast typography, primary actions, and branding elements.
- **Secondary (#725a39 / #D2B48C):** Sand / Brass. Used for warm accents, borders, and tactile highlights.
- **Neutral Light (#FCF9F2):** Ivory White. The primary canvas color, warmer and more inviting than pure digital white.
- **Surface Tiers (#F6F3EC, #F0EEE7, #EBE8E1, #E5E2DB):** Tonal warmth for layered cards, inset panels, and backdrops.

## Typography

High-contrast pairing bridging traditional heritage and modern web craft:
- **Playfair Display:** Editorial authority, craftsmanship, serif warmth for headlines and pull-quotes.
- **Montserrat:** Clean geometric counterpoint ensuring crystal-clear legibility for body, navigation, labels, and details.
